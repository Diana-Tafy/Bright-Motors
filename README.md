# 🏎️ SNXB Bright Car Sales | Business Intelligence Case Study

This project presents a comprehensive data analysis case study for SNXB Bright Car Sales, aimed at delivering actionable business intelligence to support a newly appointed **Head of Sales**.

As a **Data Analyst**, the objective was to clean, model, and analyze historical transactional data (**550,296 vehicle sales** totaling **$7.49B in revenue**) and provide strategic recommendations to optimize sales performance, inventory turnover, and dealership expansion strategies.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

This analysis focuses on answering critical executive business questions:

* **Revenue Drivers:** Which car makes and models generate the highest total revenue?
* **Valuation & Pricing Dynamics:** How do vehicle age, mileage tiers, and deal quality affect overall selling prices?
* **Regional & Territorial Performance:** Which states lead in total sales volume and overall revenue?
* **Consumer Purchasing Trends:** What vehicle attributes (e.g., body colors, price tiers) drive consumer demand?
* **Strategic Growth:** What data-backed recommendations will increase gross margins and operational efficiency?

---

## 📊 Key Executive Findings & Insights

* **Total Revenue & Volume:** **$7.49B** ($7,487,578,138) generated across 550,296 vehicle transactions, with an Average Selling Price (ASP) of **$13,606.75**.
* **Top Makes & Concentration:** **Ford** leads all manufacturers (~$1.4B in revenue), followed by Chevrolet and Nissan. The top 10 models account for **$5.32B** of total revenue.
* **Pricing & Deal Quality:** Over **98%** of transactions occurred At Market (**51.2%**) or Above Market (**46.8%**), indicating strong pricing power.
* **Regional Performance:** **Florida ($1.21B)** and **California ($1.15B)** dominate nationwide revenue, accounting for over 30% of total sales.
* **Consumer Preference (Color Breakdown):** Neutral colors dominate **71.4%** of sales volume — Black (**22.6%**), White (**20.7%**), Gray (**15.2%**), and Silver (**12.9%**).
